RLCS 2026: the season in numbers

Season 15 of the Rocket League Championship Series (RLCS) started on November 14, 2025 and ended with the World Championship, held September 15-20, 2026 at the Dickies Arena in Fort Worth, Texas. For the first time 2v2 was an official mode, alongside 3v3, which remains the main one, and 1v1, which debuted in 2025.

3v3 · Main mode

Team Falcons

Dralii, Rw9 and Kiileerrz won the final 4-0 over Spacestation Gaming and took the first world title for a team from the Middle East and Africa. Kiileerrz was named tournament MVP.

20 teams · $1,200,000 prize pool · $300,000 for the champion

2v2 · Official debut

No Miss Just Fake

Zen and Atow. won 4-3 over the twins Rw9 and Kiileerrz (Falcons) at the first 2v2 World Championship. Zen is the first player to be world champion in two different modes.

4 duos · $170,000 prize pool · $50,000 for each champion

1v1 · Second edition

nass (Gentle Mates)

Nass beat 2025 champion Nwpo 4-2 in the 1v1 World Championship final.

4 players · $85,000 prize pool

The road to Worlds

  1. Nov 14, 2025

    Season starts

    Season 15 of the RLCS begins.

  2. Dec 5-7, 2025

    Kick-Off Weekend (Copenhagen)

    New LAN event with the best of North America and Europe. Karmine Corp won, qualified for Boston and earned Europe an extra slot.

  3. Feb 19-22, 2026

    Boston Major

    Gentle Mates (Oski, nass and archie) took the title by beating Team Vitality in the final.

  4. May 20-24, 2026

    Paris Major

    Karmine Corp (Atow., Vatira and juicy) beat Twisted Minds 4-1 in front of their home crowd at La Défense Arena. Vatira was MVP.

  5. Jun-Aug 2026

    2v2 and 1v1 Opens and Last Chance Qualifiers

    Regional 2v2 and 1v1 winners qualified for Worlds, and four regions got a last chance in 3v3.

  6. Sep 15-20, 2026

    World Championship in Fort Worth

    Team Falcons beat Spacestation Gaming 4-0 in the 3v3 final.

How the RLCS 2026 3v3 works

  • Two online splits: each has three regional Opens and ends with an in-person Major.
  • Boston had 16 teams in the group stage and 8 in playoffs; Paris had 16 teams and 12 in playoffs.
  • Worlds brings together 20 teams: the 16 with the most RLCS points plus the 4 that come through the Last Chance Qualifiers. It runs with a play-in, a group stage and playoffs.
  • Seven regions: North America, Europe, South America, MENA, Oceania, Asia-Pacific and Sub-Saharan Africa.

What cars do the pros use?

  • There is no official car-usage statistic, so the figures are approximate. For the 2025 World Championship, a specialist site estimates 72% Fennec, 18% Octane and 7% Dominus.
  • Since the Fennec and the Octane share a hitbox, over 90% of pros play an Octane-hitbox car, and the Dominus is a distant third.
  • According to specialist sites, in 2025 BeastMode (NRG) played the Octane while Daniel and Atomic used the Fennec. Analyst Derek Nilsen measured 78.74% Fennec usage at an RLCS event in 2024.
  • We have not found official per-car usage data for the 2026 season.
